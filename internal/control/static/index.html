dbfailsim chaos for data

Nodes

Each card is one proxied node. Chips are the live faults on its registry (click × to remove one). Node-level faults act on the process behind the proxy through the node's target.

Inject a fault

Every kind the engine knows. Parameters come from the server's catalogue with their defaults; leave for empty to keep the fault until healed.

Scenarios

Named, timed fault sequences from config.yaml. Steps fire at their offsets; faults with a for window clear themselves.

Consistency check

Runs the same query against every node directly (bypassing the proxy) and shows where they disagree.

Fault catalogue

What each kind does, which layer it acts at, and its parameters. The same data drives the inject form and dbfailsim kinds.
